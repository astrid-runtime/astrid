Startup recovery now falls back to an older integrity-verified, lineage-valid
committed root when a newer root has an incomplete owning closure. Rejected
journal frames remain retained and are reported for diagnostics; live reads
remain fail-closed. Checksum/CAS integrity is not cryptographic authentication.
Writes after a startup fallback continue from the retained journal lineage while
the selected complete root remains the live CAS authority. Rejected-root
evidence is available through the public embedding and runtime-store diagnostic
surfaces without adding an authority-bearing wire route.
Native compaction reconciles its journal lineage with the replacement root
snapshot before acknowledging a rewrite; volume compaction fails closed while
its root-journal replacement remains unavailable.

ramdisk. The loader requires the exact length and binds `ASTRIDDC`'s signed
