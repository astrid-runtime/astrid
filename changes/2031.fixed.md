Guest IPC publication now reserves bounded interceptor queue capacity before committing an event, preserving ordered streaming deltas and terminal events during bursts instead of silently dropping them. Waiting publishers yield without blocking unrelated principal queues; cancelled reservations remain invisible, and self-dependent or circular full-queue waits are rejected. Dispatcher shutdown rejects uncommitted reservations without publishing and completes already-committed reserved handoffs in order. Captured capsule generations are fenced at commit, so retirement while waiting for capacity returns an error instead of reporting delivery to a retired runtime. Dispatcher construction refuses reserved publication until its final admission configuration is active, including reservations made before the dispatcher was installed.
