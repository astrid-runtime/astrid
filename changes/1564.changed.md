Mount admission is bound at authorize_request to the authenticated alias plus PrincipalUid and a typed MountOwnerScope. issue_lease consumes that grant, uses its UID for owner and membership decisions, and revalidates under the mutation lock that the alias still maps to the same UID, remains live, and still has the required mount capability. Bare allow_cross_owner booleans no longer cross the handler/issue_lease boundary. Failed Callback, Manifest, and Directory cleanup keep a revoked map entry until retry removes retained state.
