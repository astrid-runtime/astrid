Added a private registry-neutral package-service contract with canonical
context binding, Ed25519 authority verification, deterministic provenance
digest derivation, bounded operation history, and an executable pure
owner/package lifecycle state model.
Authority decisions bind their class, exact lifecycle plan, and exact runtime
and drain receipts. Recovery restores the exact inactive successor boundary,
and nonce-replay detection lasts only for the bounded retained record horizon.
The drain deadline binds commits after accepted proofs, drain proofs fail
closed at the exclusive authority expiry, and unknown or pre-drain records
reach conservative terminal outcomes instead of fencing a slot forever.
Admitted intents past authority expiry terminate without fencing, generation
exhaustion is preflighted at admission, drain start, and proof advance, and
the artifact budget is rechecked at commit. Test fixtures derive nonce and
signing material instead of hard-coding cryptographic values.
